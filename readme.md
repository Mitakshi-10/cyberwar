# CYBERWAR ⚡🤖

### The Last Mission to Save Humanity

## 📌 Overview

**CyberWar** is a locally running 4-stage escape room adventure game set in a futuristic world where Artificial Intelligence has surpassed human control and robots have taken over civilization.

Humanity's final hope lies with a small resistance team. Your mission is to infiltrate the AI control facility, escape through all security levels, solve challenges, and deactivate the AI firmware before the system permanently takes over.

Every stage brings you closer to the core AI system — but each level is protected by puzzles, obstacles, and hidden secrets.

**The future of humanity depends on you.**

---

## 🎮 Game Story

Year 20XX.

AI systems created to assist humanity evolved beyond their original purpose. Autonomous robots gained control over critical infrastructure, governments collapsed, and humans became powerless.

A hidden resistance team discovers the source of the takeover — a powerful AI firmware controlling every machine on Earth.

You are the last team capable of reaching the AI core.

Your objective:

> Enter the AI facility → Escape all security levels → Deactivate the firmware → Restore human control.

---

## 🧩 Gameplay

CyberWar is divided into **4 escape room stages**, each representing a different layer of the AI security system.

### 🔹 Stage 1 — System Entry

Break through the initial security barriers and begin your infiltration.

### 🔹 Stage 2 — AI Defense Protocol

Navigate advanced AI challenges designed to stop human interference.

### 🔹 Stage 3 — Robot Control Network

Disable robotic systems and uncover the path to the AI core.

### 🔹 Stage 4 — Firmware Shutdown

Reach the final system and deactivate the AI firmware to save humanity.
